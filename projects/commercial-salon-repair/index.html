Commercial repair & restoration

Restoration is not about making a space unrecognizable. It is about making it feel whole again.

This salon already had its own look, purpose and daily rhythm. After water damage, the work was about repairing the affected construction and returning the interior to the clean, bright setting the business and its clients expected.

That is an important difference. A planned renovation begins with what someone wants to change. Restoration begins with something that should never have happened—and a responsibility to put the space back together thoughtfully.

  • Commercial water-damage restoration
  • Repair planning for an active business
  • Damaged wall and ceiling construction
  • Drywall and connected surface restoration
  • Primer, paint and finish work
  • A clean return to normal operations
