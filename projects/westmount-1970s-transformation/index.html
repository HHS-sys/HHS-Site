Phased Westmount home transformation

A long-term plan, built at the right pace for the homeowners

This project has been intentionally completed in thoughtful phases rather than treated as one rushed renovation. Layout changes, demolition, drywall, painting, flooring, lighting and storage work established the direction of the home before the kitchen and later finishing phases moved forward.

The confirmed bathroom scope is one powder-room renovation. The project also includes kitchen work, cabinetry, doors, trim and related finishing. Some phases are complete; the overall transformation and kitchen are not.

  • Layout changes and demolition
  • Drywall, surface preparation and painting
  • Plank flooring and pot lights
  • Kitchen cabinetry and ongoing kitchen work
  • One powder-room renovation
  • Storage, doors, trim and finishing

Why the phased approach works

Each stage protects the bigger plan

Completing a home in phases can make the budget and disruption more manageable, as long as every decision supports the later work.

Start with function

Address layout, lighting, storage and the surfaces that influence the whole main floor.

Protect finished work

Prepare and cover completed areas while the next phase is underway.

Connect each choice

Coordinate flooring, paint, cabinetry, doors and trim with what is already complete.

Stay accurate about progress

Show completed phases clearly while staying honest about what still remains.
