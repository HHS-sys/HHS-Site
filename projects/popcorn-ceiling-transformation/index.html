Popcorn ceiling removal

The smooth finish is earned before the paint goes on.

This London ceiling project moved through multiple rooms and multiple coats. The original texture and an existing ceiling patch were assessed first. The ceiling was sanded, coated and checked in stages, with the dining room and living space progressing at different points before final sanding and primer.

The dedicated ceiling sander helped control the surface work, while drop cloths and room protection kept the process contained.

  • Existing texture and patch assessment
  • Floor, wall and opening protection
  • Mechanical ceiling sanding
  • Skim coating in controlled stages
  • Drying and surface checks
  • Final sanding, primer and paint preparation

Why preparation leads the project

Ceiling work touches the whole room.
