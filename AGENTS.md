# Hekman Home Services Website — Agent Instructions

## Mission
Build a premium, honest, photography-led website that helps homeowners and property managers choose Hekman Home Services for renovations, repairs, restoration and handyman work in London, Ontario.

The primary audience is approximately age 30–80, with special emphasis on West London: Westmount, Byron, Oakridge, Riverbend, Hyde Park, Medway and nearby neighbourhoods. The site must remain easy to read, simple to navigate and reassuring on mobile.

## Non-negotiable truth and privacy rules
- Use real Hekman project photography first. Do not use stock photography.
- Never present an AI-generated image as an actual completed project.
- AI-assisted imagery may only appear when clearly labelled `Design visualization` or `Visual reconstruction based on the completed materials and layout`.
- Do not publish client names, street numbers or complete residential addresses. Neighbourhoods and general areas may be used.
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, awards, licences, supplier partnerships, prices, warranties or credentials.
- Supplier names may be described as `materials sourced from` only when supported by the project information. Do not imply a formal partnership without evidence.
- Do not describe an ongoing project as complete. Clearly identify phased or in-progress work.
- Preserve customer dignity. Avoid unflattering language about a home or client.

## UX and accessibility standards
- Mobile-first. Test at minimum 390×844, 430×932, tablet and desktop widths.
- The mobile header and menu must never overlap, show raw desktop navigation or create horizontal scrolling.
- Use readable body text, generous line spacing and strong contrast for users up to age 80.
- Tap targets should be at least 44px high.
- Keep calls to action simple: Call, Request a Quote, View Our Work.
- Avoid excessive motion. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep photos large enough to understand the work, but curate rather than dumping every image.

## Content and conversion standards
- Lead with outcomes, practical problem-solving and trust—not generic contractor claims.
- Explain what changed functionally as well as visually.
- Show selected before, during and after stages with accurate captions.
- Use genuine short testimonials near relevant projects.
- Use short video clips only; no autoplay and no long raw uploads. Prefer 6–15 second clips or a concise 20–30 second before/during/after sequence.
- Make it clear that Rene and Steph remain close to the project and communicate directly.

## Local search and AI-discovery standards
- Write naturally for London and West London searches without keyword stuffing.
- St. Thomas is part of the service area. Mention it naturally as a nearby city served from the Westmount home base; do not imply that Hekman has a St. Thomas office.
- Use specific project pages with unique titles, descriptions, headings, alt text, captions, breadcrumbs and internal links.
- Keep `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, canonical URLs and structured data accurate.
- Mention Westmount, Byron, Oakridge, Riverbend, Hyde Park, Medway and London only where relevant.
- Prioritize useful, firsthand project detail so search engines and AI systems can understand what Hekman actually completed.

## Engineering workflow
- Do not push unreviewed redesigns directly to `main`.
- Work on a branch and open a pull request.
- Run the existing build and validation scripts.
- Check links, fragments, metadata, image paths and JavaScript syntax.
- Verify mobile and desktop rendering before requesting merge.
- Include a concise change summary, test evidence and screenshots in the pull request.
