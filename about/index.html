The Hekman approach

The people you call stay connected to the work

Hekman Home Services is owned and operated by Rene and Steph Hekman. Rene leads construction in the field. Steph guides client communication, planning and design decisions. Together they connect what is discovered on site with the choices, scope and updates that keep the project moving.

That direct involvement matters when one visible issue turns out to affect a wall, a floor or the room beside it. Decisions are explained in context, approved before extras proceed and carried through to the final details.

The Hekman Promise

Clear expectations before the work. Direct accountability throughout it

This is the standard written into our project quotes—not a slogan added after the fact.

Honest advice & transparent pricing

The scope, allowances and assumptions are explained so you can make informed decisions.

Respect for your property

Protection, an organized job site, debris removal and final cleanup are part of professional workmanship.

Approval before additional work

If you request a change or a concealed condition affects the plan, the options are discussed and approved before work proceeds.

A two-year workmanship guarantee

Installation-related defects resulting from our workmanship are covered for two years from completion.
