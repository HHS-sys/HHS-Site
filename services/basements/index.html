Common questions

Helpful before the walkthrough

The final scope depends on your property, materials and existing conditions.

Can you finish an unfinished basement?

Basement scopes can include framing, drywall, flooring, doors, trim and other finish work, subject to existing conditions and permit requirements.

Can a bathroom or kitchenette be included?

Those features can be considered when plumbing, electrical and permit requirements are addressed in the project plan.

Do you take on partial basement projects?

Yes. Work can focus on one room, flooring, ceilings, storage or another specific improvement.
