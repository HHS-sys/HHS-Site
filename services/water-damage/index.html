Common questions

Helpful before the walkthrough.

Do you stop the active leak?

The source should be identified and corrected first. Our scope can focus on removal, repair and rebuilding afterward.

Can drywall and flooring be repaired together?

Yes. One quote can include several affected finishes where appropriate.

Can you review an insurance repair scope?

We can review the requested work and prepare our own quote based on the actual site conditions and repair scope.
