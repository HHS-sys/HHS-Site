Thoughtful project planning

Built around what the space needs

Restoration work rarely begins as a planned renovation. It may start with a leak, a damaged ceiling, an opened wall or a repair that left the room unfinished. Once the source and any immediate safety concerns have been addressed, Hekman Home Services can coordinate the construction work that puts the affected space back together.

What the work can include

A complete scope, not disconnected pieces

Understand what was affected

Common questions

Helpful before the walkthrough

The final scope depends on your property, materials and existing conditions.

Do you stop the active leak?

The source should be identified and corrected first. Our scope can focus on removal, repair and rebuilding afterward.

Can drywall and flooring be repaired together?

Yes. One quote can include several affected finishes where appropriate.

Can you review an insurance repair scope?

We can review the requested work and prepare our own quote based on the actual site conditions and repair scope.
