Common questions

Helpful before the walkthrough.

The final scope depends on your property, materials and existing conditions.

Can you remove a wall?

Potential wall changes must be assessed first. Structural walls require appropriate engineering, permits and supporting work.

Can you build a new closet?

Yes. Framing, drywall, doors and trim can be included in the scope.

Do layout changes require permits?

Some do. Requirements depend on structural, plumbing, electrical and other systems affected by the work.
