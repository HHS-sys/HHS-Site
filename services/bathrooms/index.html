Common questions

Helpful before the walkthrough.

The final scope depends on your property, materials and existing conditions.

Can you renovate the entire bathroom?

Yes. Depending on the project, the scope can include demolition, preparation, waterproofing, tile, vanity, fixtures, flooring, trim and finishing.

Do you complete tub-to-shower conversions?

Yes. The existing plumbing, structure and room layout are reviewed before the conversion is quoted.

Can heated flooring be included?

Heated flooring can be considered where the floor system and overall project scope support it.
